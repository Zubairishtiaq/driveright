About DRIVERIGHT

Your trusted partner on the journey to becoming a confident, safe, and skilled driver in Reading and beyond.

Our Mission

To empower learners of all ages and backgrounds with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to drive safely and independently for life. We believe that learning to drive should be an exciting, stress-free experience, not a daunting challenge.

Our Vision

To become Reading's most trusted and recommended driving school, known for exceptional teaching standards, modern vehicles, and a genuine commitment to every student's success on the road.
